# Gate: Strategy & Change Readiness

Pass/fail/conditional checkpoint before requirements work starts.

## Checklist

- [ ] Business case has a sponsor sign-off.
- [ ] Success metrics are specific enough to fail against later (not just "improve productivity").
- [ ] Risk register covers technical, compliance, and change-adoption risk.
- [ ] Change management / communication plan exists for the affected teams.
- [ ] RACI drafted for phases 2–3.

## Recommendation

State one of:

- **Pass** — proceed to Requirements & Design.
- **Conditional pass** — proceed, with named follow-up actions and owners.
- **Fail** — do not proceed; state the specific gap and who owns closing it.

## Common failure modes

- Business case exists but has no measurable success criteria.
- Risk register only covers technical risk, missing change-adoption resistance.
- No named decision-maker for scope trade-offs during delivery.
