# Phase 1: Strategy & Planning

Establishes the business case, stakeholder alignment, and risk picture that every later phase depends on. Skipping this produces the predictable failure pattern: technology-first thinking, unclear success metrics, and scope creep once delivery starts.

## Entry criteria

- A named sponsor and a problem statement worth solving.
- Enough access to stakeholders to run an intake conversation.

## Key activities

1. **Business case & legacy assessment** — why this, why now, what it replaces.
2. **Team readiness assessment** — skills gaps, cultural readiness, resourcing.
3. **Risk assessment** — technical, operational, compliance risks and mitigations.
4. **ROI / success metrics framework** — how "worked" will be measured.
5. **Intake & prioritisation** — scope, RACI, decision rights.
6. **Change management plan** — communication and training approach for rollout.

## Exit criteria

- Business case and success metrics agreed by the sponsor.
- Risk register populated with at least technical and compliance risks.
- RACI drafted for the next two phases.

## Deliverables

Business case, risk register, RACI matrix, success-metrics framework.

## Next phase

Phase 2 — Requirements & Design, which turns this strategic frame into concrete requirements and UX flows.
