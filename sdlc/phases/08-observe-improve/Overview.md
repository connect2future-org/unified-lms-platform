# Phase 8: Observe & Improve

Turns operational data into strategic decisions: observability, analytics, performance analysis, business-impact assessment, and a prioritised improvement roadmap. Continuous, not a one-time transition — see the checkpoint doc.

## Entry criteria

- Operational data flowing from Phase 7 (monitoring, incidents, performance baselines).

## Key activities

1. **Observability** — multi-dimensional monitoring and correlation.
2. **Analytics & insights** — pattern recognition, anomaly detection.
3. **Performance analysis** — bottleneck identification and optimisation.
4. **Business impact assessment** — correlating technical metrics to business outcomes.
5. **Strategic improvement planning** — prioritised, resourced roadmap.
6. **Continuous improvement execution** — implementing and tracking the roadmap.

## Deliverables

Observability dashboards, business-impact analysis, prioritised improvement roadmap.

## Next phase

Phase 9 — Maintain & Evolve, which executes long-horizon maintenance and architectural evolution.
