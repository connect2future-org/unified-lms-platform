# Checkpoint: Improvement

Continuous operational work, not a one-time transition — no pass/fail gate. Review periodically (recommended: monthly) against the questions below.

## Review questions

- What did observability actually surface this period that we didn't already know?
- Which improvement items on the roadmap moved, and which stalled — why?
- Is there a measurable link between the last improvement shipped and a business outcome, or is impact still assumed?
- Are we spending improvement capacity on the highest-impact items, or the easiest ones?

## Why no hard gate

Gates model one-time pass/fail transitions between phases. Improvement work is a continuous loop — forcing a pass/fail checkpoint here would be fake precision. Use this checkpoint to structure a recurring review, not to block anything.

## Escalation trigger

If the roadmap consistently stalls on the same item, or an improvement is blocked by an architectural constraint, escalate into Phase 9 (Maintain & Evolve) as a candidate for strategic evolution rather than leaving it as a recurring stuck item.
