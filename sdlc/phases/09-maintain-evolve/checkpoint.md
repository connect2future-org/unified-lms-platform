# Checkpoint: Evolution

Continuous operational work, not a one-time transition — no pass/fail gate. Review periodically (recommended: quarterly) against the questions below.

## Review questions

- Is the technical debt register growing or shrinking relative to delivery pace?
- Are there dependencies or platforms approaching end-of-life that need a modernisation decision now rather than reactively?
- Is knowledge concentrated in one or two people ("tribal knowledge") for anything critical?
- Has anything from this period's maintenance work surfaced a case for revisiting an architectural decision (loop back to Phase 3) or a strategic assumption (loop back to Phase 1)?

## Why no hard gate

Gates model one-time pass/fail transitions between phases. Maintenance and evolution is an ongoing cycle — forcing a pass/fail checkpoint here would be fake precision. Use this checkpoint to structure a recurring review, not to block anything.

## Escalation trigger

If technical debt review surfaces a change significant enough to need new strategic sign-off (budget, scope, or direction), open a new Phase 1 intake rather than treating it as routine maintenance.
