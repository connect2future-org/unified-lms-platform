# Phase 2: Requirements & Design

Turns business need into specific, testable requirements and UX flows. Rushing this phase is the single biggest source of expensive rework later — ambiguous requirements produce solutions that miss the business objective.

## Entry criteria

- Business case and success metrics agreed (Phase 1 gate passed).

## Key activities

1. **Requirements gathering** — stakeholder interviews, gap identification, consolidation.
2. **User story development** — personas, epics, story sizing.
3. **UX flows** — journey mapping, interaction patterns, accessibility.
4. **Acceptance criteria definition** — functional and non-functional requirements, quality gates.
5. **Design validation & stakeholder approval** — multi-stakeholder review before development starts.

## Exit criteria

- Requirements traced to business objectives, with acceptance criteria attached.
- Stakeholder approval recorded across business, technical, security, and QA reviewers.

## Deliverables

Requirements specification, user stories with acceptance criteria, UX flows, design validation record.

## Next phase

Phase 3 — Architecture & Design, which turns approved requirements into a system design.
